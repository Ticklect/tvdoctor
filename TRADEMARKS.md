# TVDoctor trademark policy

TVDoctor names, logos, and branding identify the TVDoctor project and its
official releases. The software licence does not grant trademark rights.

You may use the TVDoctor name to refer truthfully to this project, to describe
compatibility, or to identify an unmodified official release. Do not use the
name or branding in a way that suggests sponsorship, endorsement, or an
official TVDoctor release when none exists.

Forks and modified distributions should use a distinct product name and branding
and clearly state that they are derived from TVDoctor. This policy does not limit
uses permitted by applicable trademark law.
